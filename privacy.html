Runwake

Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Runwake is designed so your mornings stay on your iPhone. We do not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Runwake has no account, no server and no analytics, advertising or tracking code. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Your alarms, runway steps, mornings, progress, priorities and settings are stored only on your iPhone. A small summary (your next alarm time, whether it is ready, and your streak) is shared with Runwake's own widgets on the same device. Deleting an alarm removes it; deleting the app removes all of this data.

Permissions

Each permission is requested only when you use the feature that needs it, and everything is processed on your device:

  • Alarms — to schedule your wake-up alarms, follow-up rings and runway checks with the system alarm.
  • Camera — only during the Scan and Squats missions, to read a code you registered or count squats. Video is never saved or sent. For Scan, only the code's text is stored.
  • Motion — only during the Steps mission, to count steps. Step data is not stored.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — only during the Say it mission, to check you said your phrase. Recognition runs on your iPhone; audio is not saved or sent.
  • Calendars (optional) — to show your next event when your morning lands. Events are read on your iPhone and are not stored or sent.
  • Notifications (optional) — for bedtime and weekly recap reminders you turn on.

Purchases

Runwake Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Payments are handled by Apple; Runwake only learns whether Pro is active on your Apple Account. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Runwake does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
