Support

My alarm didn't ring

  • Open Runwake → Today → tap the status line to see Alarm readiness, and ring a test alarm.
  • Make sure Alarms are allowed for Runwake in Settings, Runwake isn't hidden or locked, and your alarm volume isn't set very low in Settings › Sounds & Haptics.
  • Open Runwake at least every couple of days so follow-up rings stay prepared.

Why can I still stop the alarm?

iOS always lets you stop a ringing alarm. Instead of blocking it, Runwake rings again a few minutes later until you finish your mission, a limited number of times.

I'm stuck in a mission

Hold “I'm up, end this morning” for 3 seconds at any time. If a mission can't run, Runwake switches to Math.

Restore purchases

Settings → Restore Purchases. Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
